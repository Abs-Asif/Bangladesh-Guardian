PRIVACY POLICY

1. DATA COLLECTION
This application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data of its users to external servers. It is a browser-based tool that operates within the user's local environment.

2. LOCAL STORAGE
- IndexedDB: The application uses IndexedDB (SecretBGDB) to store a history of generated photocards locally on your device for your convenience.
- LocalStorage: Settings such as language preference, auto-active state, and notification audio selection are saved in your browser's LocalStorage.

3. EXTERNAL DATA FETCHING
To provide latest news updates, the app connects to the Bangladesh Guardian API. It also uses public CORS proxies to fetch images and metadata from the news site to circumvent cross-origin restrictions. No user identification data is sent during these requests.

4. SECURITY
Access to the application is protected by a security key. This key is processed locally and is not sent to any server.

5. UPDATES
As this is a proprietary tool, updates are managed internally by the newspaper's technical team.
